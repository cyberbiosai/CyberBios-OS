# Open Source CyberBios OS & Pythagorean Academy Architecture

## Executive Overview
CyberBios OS is an Open Source Eco Energy Saving System engineered to provide autonomous energy monitoring, automated load shedding, and data-backed management across residential, commercial, and industrial infrastructure.

Under the academic and educational oversight of the **Open Source Pythagorean Academy**, CyberBios OS provides AI tooling, open-source firmware, and official developer certifications to build a global, decentralized energy-saving ecosystem.

---

## The 3-Tier Business Architecture

### TIER 1: COMMONS & ACADEMY (Open Source Core)
* **Institutional Sponsor:** Open Source Pythagorean Academy
* **Core OS:** Open Source CyberBios OS (Eco Energy Saving System)
* **Hardware Anchor:** **Cyber Πέτρα (Cyber Stone)**
  * Low-CAPEX / Symbolic cost distribution for residential users and vulnerable households.
  * BYOD (Bring Your Own Device) integration via simple DIY instructions for existing smart plugs and meters.
* **AI Intelligence:** **CyberBios Agent 333** (Advanced LLM Model)
  * Ingests physical telemetry from the Cyber Πέτρα and connected devices.
  * Responds with precise, data-backed proof and actionable energy-saving instructions.
* **Ecosystem Role:** Developer engagement (IBM open-source strategy), AI training workshops, and official academy certifications.

---

### TIER 2: PROFESSIONAL / PRO-SUMER (Retail Group, HoReCa & SMEs)
* **Primary Target Groups:**
  1. **Retail Group:** Retail stores and commercial chain networks (Smart Energy Monitoring).
  2. **HoReCa:** Hotels, Restaurants, Catering, Cafe-Bars.
  3. **Commercial SMEs:** Smart Airbnb properties, offices, and small/medium businesses.
* **Hardware Suite:** **Cyber Πέτρα** + **Cyber Πλέγμα (Window Mesh)** + Custom Smart Actuators.
* **SLA & Integration:** Custom installation, circuit adjustments, and technical auditing upon direct engagement.
* **Core Value Driver:** **Aggressive Energy Cut Loop**
  * Automated load shedding during peak hours and idle periods.
  * Occupancy-driven climate and power scenarios (Airbnb / Retail Night Mode).
* **Scale Policy:** High-scale Retail Groups naturally upgrade into Tier 3 Enterprise infrastructure.

---

### TIER 3: ENTERPRISE & HEAVY INDUSTRY (The Capital & Profit Engine)
* **Target Group:** Heavy Industry, Manufacturing Plants, Large Warehouses, Mega Retail Chains.
* **Onboarding SLA:** Mandatory On-Site Energy Flow Audit & Appliance Inventory Mapping.
* **Hardware & Compute Setup:** Industrial Grade Sovereign Node Clusters (Edge AI, ROS 2, NVIDIA Jetson).
* **Core Value Driver:** Zero-Trust AI Autonomy, Real-time Industrial Process Optimization, and Air-Gapped Data Sovereignty.
* **Financial Engine:** High-margin Enterprise B2B revenues fully fund Tier 1 community operations and Pythagorean Academy R&D.

## Hardware Specifications Overview (High-Level Functional Specs)

### 1. Cyber Πέτρα (Cyber Stone) - Tier 1 & Tier 2 Anchor
* **Microcontroller:** ESP32 / ESP32-C3 Architecture (MicroPython Core).
* **Sensors:** Non-invasive CT Clamp (Current Transformer), Ambient Temp/Humidity, Mains Voltage Sensor.
* **Connectivity:** Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n, BLE 5.0, MQTT over TLS.

### 2. Cyber Πλέγμα (Window Mesh) - Tier 2 Actuation Node
* **Actuators:** Smart Relays, PWM Dimmer Interfaces, Window Shade Actuators.
* **Bus Architecture:** RS485 / Modbus RTU / Zigbee Mesh Protocol.
